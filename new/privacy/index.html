Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

This policy explains how OTMOS may handle information shared through website enquiries, email conversations, program applications, employer discussions and recruitment activity.

Information you choose to share

This may include your name, contact details, organisation, professional profile, business requirements, application information and other details you voluntarily provide.

How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, assess program or career interest, understand business requirements, operate relevant services, maintain records and meet applicable legal obligations.

Sharing and retention

OTMOS does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared with authorised service providers or relevant business participants only where required for the stated purpose, subject to appropriate controls. Records are retained only as reasonably needed for operational, legal or legitimate business purposes.

Your questions

For privacy questions or a request concerning information you have shared, email azad@otmos.io.
