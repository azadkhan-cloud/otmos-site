OTMOS Divisions

One ecosystem. Five clear responsibilities.

The divisions work together where useful, but they are not the same service. Each one has its own audience, purpose and operating boundary.

Business transformation and autonomous systems
AUTONOMOUS · BY OTMOS

Turn manual, people-dependent operations into connected business systems.

OTMOS Autonomous helps organisations understand operations, redesign processes and introduce focused automation. Improvements begin with a clear business need and keep important decisions under human control.

Process assessment · Workflow redesign · Focused automation · Operational visibility

Professional and organisational capability
ACADEMY · BY OTMOS

Build AI-compatible professionals through practical, domain-specific learning.

OTMOS Academy helps professionals apply AI and automation to real work. Its application-first model combines practical workflows, projects, assessment and workforce-readiness support for individuals and organisations.

Practical learning · Domain workflows · Projects · Corporate capability

AI-compatible talent connection
HIROS · BY OTMOS

Help employers connect with assessed, job-ready professional talent.

OTMOS HIROS is being developed as an employer-focused talent platform connected to OTMOS Academy. Structured evidence can support employer review, while selection and employment decisions remain with the organisation.

Employer focused · Assessed talent · Human-controlled hiring · Talent network

Connected HR operations
AIHRMS · BY OTMOS

Create a practical HR foundation with clearer records, processes and visibility.

OTMOS AIHRMS is being developed progressively, beginning with a practical Pilot and Level 1 HR-operations foundation. Its direction is to connect essential workforce information, recurring processes, requests, documents and reporting.
